SoFaygo

Andre Dontrel Burt Jr. (born October 3, 2001), better known by his stage name SoFaygo, is an American rapper and singer. His 2019 single, "Knock Knock", went viral on TikTok in late 2020 before receiving platinum certification by the RIAA. In 2021, he featured on Trippie Redd's song "MP5", marking his first entry on the Billboard Hot 100. He later signed with Travis Scott's Cactus Jack Records and released his debut studio album, Pink Heartz (2022).
